THE OTHER TIMELINE - READ ME FIRST
==================================

WHAT THIS IS
This program builds The Other Timeline videos on this PC.
It uploads a video to YouTube ONLY after you press Approve.

INSTALL (one time)
1. Unzip this folder into your Documents folder.
2. Open the folder and double-click INSTALL.bat.
3. Keep the black window open. It takes 10-20 minutes.
4. When it asks "Optimize this PC?", press Y.
5. Windows asks for permission. Click Yes.
6. At the end, the dashboard opens in your browser.

FIRST-TIME SIGN-IN (one time)
1. On the dashboard, click "Open YouTube to sign in".
2. A Chrome window opens.
3. Sign in to the channel's Google account in that window.
4. Leave that Chrome window open.
5. Never sign out of it or delete its profile.

DAILY USE
1. Copy a video folder from the Mac into the "videos" folder.
2. Put the voice recording inside that video folder.
   Name it voice.wav, voice.mp3 or voice.m4a.
3. The build starts by itself. The dashboard shows progress.
4. A 15-minute video can take 1-3 hours on this PC.
   Leave the PC plugged in and switched on.
5. When it says "Ready to check", click "Watch video".
6. If it looks good, click "Approve & upload".
7. The video goes up as Private.
8. Open YouTube Studio and publish it when you are ready.

WHAT EACH STATUS MEANS
Waiting for voice   The folder has no voice file yet.
Queued              Waiting its turn to build.
Building            Working on it. Leave the PC on.
Ready to check      Finished. Watch it, then approve.
Approved            Will upload next.
Uploading           Sending to YouTube. Do not close Chrome.
Uploaded            On YouTube as Private. Publish it in Studio.
Something went wrong / Upload failed
                    See TROUBLESHOOTING below.

TROUBLESHOOTING
- Dashboard won't open:
  Double-click "The Other Timeline" icon on the desktop.
- "Something went wrong":
  Click Retry. If it fails again, copy the Activity log
  from the dashboard and send it to Jitheender.
- A Google sign-in page appears:
  Sign in again in the Chrome window, then click Retry.
- "Daily upload limit":
  YouTube limits uploads per day. Wait 24 hours, then retry.
- Disk almost full:
  Delete old folders in "videos" once they are on YouTube.

UNDO THE PC TUNE-UP
Right-click optimize_pc_undo.ps1 and choose "Run with PowerShell".
